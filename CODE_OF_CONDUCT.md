# Code of Conduct

Halos adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its Code of Conduct. The full text at that link is the authoritative version; this file summarizes it.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be welcoming, empathetic and respectful.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community, not just for you.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This Code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) and when representing the project in public. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that violate it, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors.

Report violations privately to **conduct@halos.dev** (placeholder: replace with the real address before the first public release). All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Enforcement follows the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines): correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.
